BRO-B-Q

The Charter

The traditions that guide every gathering.

I

Community Comes First

BRO-B-Q celebrates friendships built throughout the year. The event does not create the community. The community creates the event.

II

Attendance Is Earned, Never Assumed

An invitation one year does not guarantee an invitation the next. The people around the pit are there because they remain part of the community.

III

Everyone Contributes

Food. Drinks. Cigars. Time. Stories. Laughter. Everyone brings something that makes the day better.

IV

Craft Matters

Good barbecue cannot be rushed. Neither can tradition. Take pride in the details and let the smoke do its work.

V

Family Is Always Welcome

Family is an automatic invitation. They are part of the story, even when they only stay for part of the day.

VI

The Brokayest Bro Decides

The reigning Brokayest Bro names the next. No criteria are required. No explanation is owed. Vibes remain sufficient.

VII

Leave Something Behind

A better dish. A new story. A ridiculous jersey. A stronger friendship. Every chapter should leave the next one something worth keeping.

The smoke clears.

The tradition stays.

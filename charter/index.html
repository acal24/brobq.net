BRO-B-Q

The Charter

The traditions that guide.

I

Community Comes First

BRO-B-Q celebrates friendships built throughout the year. The event does not create bros. The bros create the event.

II

Attendance Is Earned, Never Assumed

An invitation one year does not guarantee an invitation the next. The bros at this herf are there because they remain committed to the community.

III

Everyone Contributes

Food. Drinks. Cigars. Time. Stories. Laughter. Everyone brings something that makes the day better.

IV

Craft Matters

Good barbecue cannot be rushed. Neither can tradition. Take pride in the details and let the smoke do its work.

V

Family Is Always Welcome

Family is an automatic invitation. They are part of the story.

VI

The Brokayest Bro Decides

The reigning Brokayest Bro names the next. Never any criteria, ever. No explanation is owed. Vibes only.

VII

Make Your Mark

A dish. A new story. A ridiculous jersey. A stronger friendship. Every chapter should leave the next one something worth keeping.

The smoke clears.

The tradition stays.
